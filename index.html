Selected work

Custom Macropad

A small keyboard of my own: three keys and a knob that trigger shortcuts and control volume on my Mac. I soldered the parts, built the acrylic case and programmed what each key does.

My Learning Roadmap

A one-page map of the skills I have, the ones I want next, and how they lead to a Master's degree. I made it after studying how products like the Philips Hue Bridge and the Oura Ring are built.

Memory Mirror

An installation that plays with a live camera image. Turning the knobs makes the picture more blocky or more colourful, like a memory fading in and out.

ArduinoProcessingLive video

Kallax Smart Bin

A bin that knows when it is full and when it starts to smell. Sensors check how full it is and how fresh the air is, then send a wireless message so it only gets emptied when needed.

SensorsWirelessArduino

Da Vinci Tank

A wooden armoured vehicle based on Leonardo da Vinci's 1487 sketch. I modelled and textured it in Blender, then made it drivable in the browser.

Tatami Room

A traditional Japanese room built in 3D, with sliding paper screens and woven floor mats. I focused on proportions, natural light and believable materials.
