1/6 Electronics, year 2

Custom Macropad

My own small keyboard: three keys and a knob that run shortcuts and control the volume on my Mac.

I started from loose parts: a circuit board, switches, an acrylic case, screws and a spool of solder. I soldered the board and switches myself, stacked the layered acrylic case around it, and programmed what each key and the knob does.

What I did
Soldering, assembly, key programming
Made with
Soldering iron, mechanical switches, acrylic

2/6 Interactive installation, year 1

Memory Mirror

A live camera image that you can fade into a memory by turning knobs.

Knobs wired to an Arduino control a Processing sketch that runs on the live webcam feed. Turn one way and the picture breaks into bigger and bigger blocks; turn another and the colours drain or flare, like a memory fading in and out. The particles here do the same thing.

What I did
Circuit, Arduino code, Processing sketch
Made with
Arduino, potentiometers, Processing, webcam

3/6 Sensors and IoT, year 1

Kallax Smart Bin

A bin that knows when it is full and when it starts to smell.

Sensors under the wooden lid measure how full the bin is and how fresh the air inside is. When either one crosses a limit, the bin sends a wireless message, so it gets emptied when it needs to be instead of on a fixed schedule.

What I did
Sensor wiring, thresholds, wireless messages
Made with
Arduino, fill and air sensors, wireless module

4/6 3D and game, year 1

Da Vinci Tank

Leonardo da Vinci's armoured vehicle from around 1487, modelled in Blender and drivable in your browser.

I rebuilt the sketch as a 3D model: a ribbed wooden shell with cannons on every side, riding on a wheeled frame. I textured it with wood materials, then turned it into a small playable demo you can drive around.

What I did
Modelling, texturing, playable build
Made with
Blender, browser game export
Play it on itch.io

5/6 3D, year 1

Tatami Room

A traditional Japanese room with a sweeping roof, built in Blender.

Paper-screen walls in a strict grid, a curved tiled roof and woven tatami mats on the floor. The project was about restraint: getting proportions, soft natural light and believable materials right without adding clutter.

What I did
Modelling, lighting, materials
Made with
Blender

6/6 Creative coding, year 1

Creature Program

An animated beach where creatures react to your clicks and throw a ball to each other.

Written in Java with Processing and inspired by Lilo & Stitch. Every creature is drawn from code, the ocean keeps moving, clicking a creature makes it react, and you can pass the red ball from one to the next.

What I did
Drawing, animation and interaction in code
Made with
Java, Processing

About

I'm Oliwer, a second-year Creative Technology student at VU Amsterdam. I learn by building: I would rather get a rough prototype working than read about it first.

Most of my projects sit where hardware, code and design meet. Outside the studio I'm hooked on astrophysics (how stars, orbits and black holes move together), history and geopolitics. That's where the galaxy at the top of this page comes from. The cube behind this text is a nod to the first thing I ever soldered: an LED cube.
